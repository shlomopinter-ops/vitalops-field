Common questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are created by your administrator. There is no public sign-up.

I forgot my password.
Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and follow the email link.

How do I turn on Face ID?
Sign in with your password once and tap "Use Face ID" when asked. You can turn it off any time in My Settings.

I'm not getting notifications.
Open iPhone Settings › Notifications › VitalOps Field and make sure notifications are allowed.

How do I delete my account or data?
Email us at the address above and we will handle the request.
